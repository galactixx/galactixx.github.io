About

My research interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing and large language models. I am particularly interested in how model predictions are affected by prompts and how to evaluate these systems more reliably.

In my recent work at EMNLP 2026, we examine and diagnose a conservative bias in zero-shot authorship verification and investigate lightweight prompt mitigation methods.

Research interests

I am interested in developing more reliable ways to understand, evaluate, and improve language model behavior.
